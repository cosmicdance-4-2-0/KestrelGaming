A team is activated only when the host **actually loads** its contract, resolves each member's real home revision, and establishes authorized task scope. Neither this catalog nor the team name starts a multi-agent runtime.

Until controlled comparative trials are completed, the team should be described as *structurally ready for manual composite use*, **not** proven better than generic Kestrel or any constituent agent.
