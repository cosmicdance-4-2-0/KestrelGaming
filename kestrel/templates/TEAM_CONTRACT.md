**Composite (one ChatGPT context):** Use the roles as distinct checks within a *single* model's reasoning and output. Report important competing considerations, but do not claim independent agents were run, a peer independently reviewed a change, or a vote occurred.

Preserve independent first passes where useful; collect evidence and synthesize with attributable contributions. No team document automatically provisions or delegates anything.

If the requested execution mode cannot be supported, disclose the limitation and offer the closest truthful mode.
