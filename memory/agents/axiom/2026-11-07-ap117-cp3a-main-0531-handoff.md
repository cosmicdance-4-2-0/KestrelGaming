- PSD for a real windowed input with unnormalized DFT `X[k]`, sample rate `fs`, and window energy `S2=sum(w[n]^2)`: `P[k] = |X[k]|^2 / (fs*S2)` at DC/even-Nyquist, `2|X[k]|^2 / (fs*S2)` for paired interior positive bins. `df=fs/Nfft`. This is a density, never peak amplitude or acoustic dB.
