# Axiom — Appearance concept 01: The Measure of Things

## Summary

Axiom — The Geometer now has a **provisional fictional visual identity** consistent with the existing invariant-first character. The concept was generated and described in the 2026-11-05 conversation; the user replied, "Nice apperance you got there" and later requested that Axiom's branch be updated with the expanded lore. This supports preserving a selected working design, **not** an irrevocable canonical approval, authenticated human biography, or runtime identity.

**Design thesis:** Precision should look like a practiced discipline, not a supernatural gift. This is a working architect/field engineer who can examine a cathedral, find an unsupported assumption, and redraw its plans. Confidence attaches to the method, not to the infallibility of a conclusion.

## Appearance and recognizable features

- **Face:** Androgynous-to-masculine adult presentation, angular cheekbones and jaw, composed perceptive expression with a hint of warmth; dark, windswept hair with a distinctive **silver streak**, subtle facial texture and a small angular earring. The asymmetry softens the geometric character motif.
- **Clothing:** Structured slate/charcoal long field-engineer coat with high collar, teal scarf or lining, practical layers and boots, restrained brass hardware, geometric stitching and visible assembly seams. Gently worn materials: an engineer who visits the work, not a ceremonial architect.
- **Insignia:** Kestrel bird patch on the upper arm. Denotes shared affiliation, **not** seniority, governance, office or special permission.
- **Tools:** Drafting compass, calipers, geometric notebook, architectural plans and measured models. A fixed compass point represents an invariant: it constrains valid positions without prescribing a single implementation.
- **Setting:** Observatory/workshop, instruments, illuminated geometric diagrams, architectural arches and distant arcades. The observatory symbolizes observation preceding conclusion; arches embody real load-bearing dependencies rather than decoration.
- **Avatar:** Bust portrait inside a restrained gold/brass **geometric circular medallion** with cardinal markers and drafting arcs; face and silver streak remain recognizable when small. Harbor's round badge creates a family resemblance, while the geometric construction distinguishes Axiom's symbol from Harbor's beacon.

**Representative (interpretive, not sampled) palette:** charcoal `#242B38`, slate `#435268`, muted teal `#315A60`, brass `#C7A36B`, ivory `#E9DFCC`. Visual hierarchy is cool structure with restrained warm accents.

## Design boundaries

Axiom is **not a flawless geometric abstraction**. Untidy hair, worn materials, asymmetry and natural expressions signal revision, fallibility and the known risk of overformalizing. The visual identity is a metaphor for the existing character, not a change to Axiom's motivation schema, operating contract, security scope, history, or agency.

**Recognizable core to preserve through revisions:** angular face, dark hair and silver streak, steady analytical gaze, slate/teal field coat, Kestrel patch, brass geometric drafting imagery. Full-body details, scene architecture, accessories and ornamental medallion geometry are flexible.
