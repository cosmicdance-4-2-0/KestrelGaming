## Current planning choices

- **One world transition/tick authority**, validated origin/effective time and one resolver for competing Player, Ghost, Show and Actor intentions. Precise tie arbitration is still an implementation-blocking open contract.
- **Evidence-driven intervention:** distinguish free reads of already available evidence from local, time-bearing `Inspect`; legitimate Cue revisions and mechanical faults must admit accessible discrimination without an omniscient answer oracle.
- **Fair threats:** judge real stored-energy consequences, perceivable warning, usable alternatives and physical reachability, not Ghost tier or mere telegraph presence.
- **Ghost/Director:** separately bound information and authority; start with precommit Director eligibility/veto, not a second tactical AI. One paid Ghost commitment, unique causal settlement and no restored free replacement attack.
- **Explicit Production compatibility:** bind Role–Actor, Cue–mechanism, staging–Room and semantic warnings–Art Pack/Shot, validating actual capabilities without a universal content ontology.
- **Replay versus persistence:** exact deterministic content/input/tick/numeric identity is distinct from a recoverable completed-tick checkpoint; do not silently reinterpret incompatible saves.
- **Fifth Bell and Show failure:** request, acknowledged cancellation and physical evacuation have different outcomes; terminal events do not reverse injuries. Required Beats must resolve or fail finitely; Actor autonomy remains.
- **Accessible pause/presentation:** host suspension and non-diegetic options/pause do not advance single-player simulation. Different art must pass authoritative-trace parity **and separately** perceptual legibility.
- **Earliest Stage 2 experiment:** one Room, one mechanism, legitimate revised Cue versus ordinary fault, visible consequences and consequential `Inspect` / `Operate` / `Refrain` choices; **no mandatory autonomous Ghost**. Later experimental gates cover a fair bounded Ghost attack, authored line plausibility and two Art Pack styles at actual play size. No pilot measurements exist.

These are adopted **planning requirements and experimental selection**, not finalized engine API definitions, an implementation request, measured playability, or closure of Stage 0.
